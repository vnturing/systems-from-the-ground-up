## Build storage strength
Immediate fluency can feel like mastery. A stronger test is explaining a new case after a delay. Start in course order, then mix practice across related skills: a pointer bound, a byte-order decode, a cache mapping, and a short-write trace.

The review buttons use a simple transparent schedule. “Still uncertain” schedules tomorrow and resets the interval. “Recalled unaided” advances through 1, 3, 7, 14, and 30 days. Review dates are reminders, not a scientific assessment of mastery. No reminders run outside this website and no scheduled automation is created.

## Use your own evidence
Completion is optional and reversible. A quiz check records an attempt in this browser; a correct answer can be a guess. Reflections and lab reports provide richer evidence to discuss with the teacher. Do not mark a new lesson complete solely to improve the percentage.

The tools below require JavaScript. The curriculum, readings, retrieval prompts, and revealable worked solutions remain available without it. If storage is blocked or full, the page will tell you and continue in session memory; export before leaving to keep that record.
